First research question · General Dynamics

If new aid to Ukraine slows, how much replenishment work continues?

A slowdown in new aid raises two different questions for an investor. How much work has the government already committed to General Dynamics? How much future business depends on the Army placing another order?

This case will follow one award from its initial funding through later revisions, then estimate how much a follow-on order could change the value of one GD share.
